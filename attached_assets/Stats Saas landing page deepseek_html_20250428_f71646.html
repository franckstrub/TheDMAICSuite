The Complete DMAIC Framework

Our platform is structured around the proven DMAIC methodology to guide your projects to success

1

Define

Clearly articulate the problem, project scope, goals, and customer requirements.

2

Measure

Collect data and establish baseline performance of the current process.

3

Analyze

Identify root causes of defects or inefficiencies through data analysis.

4

Improve

Develop, test, and implement solutions to address root causes.

5

Control

Sustain the gains by standardizing processes and implementing controls.
